Current architecture

A pipe, not a dependency.

The current Rust line is being validated as an independent reversible transform. Rare1 can sit after DU3, before a model, or stay out of the pipeline entirely. The surrounding stages remain replaceable.

Global correctness and bounded execution are separate concerns: the logical decision can cover the declared scope while implementation remains chunk-aware and memory-bounded.
