Research question

Can an encoded artifact remain experiential?

ZXSynth treats output as something a human can perceive, not just a byte blob. That makes it useful as a playground for deterministic synthesis, carrier design and reversible media experiments.

Boundary

Not a security primitive.

Obfuscation, encryption and reversibility are separate properties. Any release should state exactly which one it proves.

Build
