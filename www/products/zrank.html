Experimental module / model pipe

zRank
model context. Keep entropy optional.

zRank is not the compressor at the end of the line. It is an experimental reversible model transform: consume an ID stream, emit a rank-oriented representation plus model state, then hand the result to whatever downstream coder earns its place.

Module boundary

A model plug, not another monolith.

Current research treats model build, encode, decode, sidebands and entropy as separate costs. That keeps zRank useful even when the downstream coder changes.

Model/rank size, build cost, inverse cost and entropy response are measured separately. A smaller intermediate is not silently called a complete archive.
