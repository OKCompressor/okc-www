Why it exists

Make representation part of the interface.

Pongo explores what happens when encoded state is inspectable as an image-like object instead of being hidden inside an opaque archive. It is useful as a falsifiable mechanism and as a source of ideas for custom carriers.

Boundary

Not steganographic security.

Visibility, reversibility and concealment are different claims. Pongo only claims the first two where a release proves them.
